Common Questions

Everything you should know before joining

Are there any security deposits or hidden setup fees?

No. Our pricing is completely transparent. Month-to-month plans require no long-term lease lock-in or surprise maintenance overheads.

Can I really access the space at 3:00 AM?

Yes, absolutely. Space Station is truly operational 24/7/365. Monthly members receive biometric entry access so you can work during international time zones or whenever inspiration strikes.

What happens if K-Electric or LESCO cuts the power?

Our heavy-duty generator backup and industrial online UPS units activate instantaneously. You will never experience dropped video calls, rebooting routers, or power fluctuations.

Can I test the space before committing to a monthly plan?

Yes. You can either book a complimentary guided tour with our community team or purchase a Day Pass (PKR 900) to test the ergonomics and fiber speed for a full working day.
